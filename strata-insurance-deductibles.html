Strata insurance deductibles and how much personal coverage you need

How strata building deductibles can be charged back to owners, the earthquake deductible problem, and how to size your condo policy's deductible coverage.

A strata corporation must insure the building for full replacement value. Since 2019, premiums and deductibles in BC have climbed steeply, and the deductibles are where individual owners carry real risk. If damage starts in your unit, or if a big event hits the whole building, part of that deductible can land on you.

Typical deductibles today

Earthquake deductibles are different: they are usually a percentage of the building value, commonly 10% to 20%. On a building insured for $40,000,000 with a 15% earthquake deductible, the deductible is $6,000,000. Shared across 60 units by unit entitlement, that averages $100,000 per unit.

When the deductible is charged to you

Under the Strata Property Act, a strata can recover the deductible from an owner who is responsible for the loss, and many bylaws make owners responsible when damage originates in their unit, whether or not they were negligent. A failed dishwasher hose that floods three floors can trigger a $100,000 water deductible charged to the owner of the unit where it started.

For building-wide events such as an earthquake, the deductible is a common expense. Owners pay it as a special levy, shared by unit entitlement.

Sizing your condo insurance

A condo (HO-6) policy covers your contents, improvements, liability and, importantly, loss assessment or strata deductible coverage. Match that coverage to the building's largest deductible you could be charged:

Building deductibleSuggested personal deductible coverage
Water: $100,000At least $100,000
Earthquake: 15% of valueYour unit-entitlement share, often $50,000 to $150,000
Sewer back-up: $50,000At least $50,000

Earthquake deductible coverage is usually a separate endorsement and costs more. Ask your insurer for a quote using your unit entitlement share from the strata plan.

Reading the insurance certificate

The strata's certificate of insurance lists insurer, coverage amounts and every deductible. Request it with the Form B when buying, and check it at each renewal. A building that changed insurers or had several claims may see deductibles jump mid-ownership.

Reducing water claims

Most strata claims are water. Many buildings now require owners to replace washing machine hoses with braided steel, install leak detectors, or shut off water when away. Those bylaws exist because one leak can raise everyone's premiums.

Questions people ask

Can the strata charge me the full deductible?

If your bylaws and the Act make you responsible for the loss, the strata can charge back up to the deductible amount.

Does my condo insurance cover the strata deductible?

Only if it includes deductible or loss assessment coverage, and only up to the limit you choose.

Is earthquake coverage included?

Strata policies usually include earthquake with a large percentage deductible; personal coverage for that deductible is extra.

Where do I find the deductibles?

On the strata's certificate of insurance, available from the strata manager.
